## 2. Four School Houses (Core Student Culture)

The school's co-curricular and leadership development is anchored in four official houses, each named after an intellectual and humanitarian colossus:

1. **Teresa House (Blue)**
   - **Patron:** Mother Teresa
   - **Core Values:** Compassion, empathy, humility, and selfless community service
   - **Role in UI:** Electric & Sapphire Blue accents; community outreach, pre-primary care
2. **Amartya House (Yellow)**
   - **Patron:** Nobel Laureate Amartya Sen
   - **Core Values:** Wisdom, intellectual inquiry, economic thought, and human capability development
   - **Role in UI:** Solar Gold & Amber accents; academic excellence, scholarships, debate
3. **Tagore House (Green)**
   - **Patron:** Gurudev Rabindranath Tagore
   - **Core Values:** Artistic expression, literature, poetry, and communion with nature
   - **Role in UI:** Emerald & Botanical Green; creative arts, environment, cultural assemblies
4. **Raman House (Red)**
   - **Patron:** Nobel Laureate Sir C.V. Raman
   - **Core Values:** Scientific spirit, relentless inquiry, athletic vigour, and courage
   - **Role in UI:** Crimson & Vermilion Red; laboratory science, sports championships, high-intent CTAs

## 3. Verified Academic & Examination Structure

- **Teaching Methodology Pillars:**
  - *The Flight Path:* Harmonious partnership among Management, Parents, Staff, and Students.
  - *Different Strokes:* Multiple intelligence framework providing varied learning stimuli for individual needs.
  - *Joy of Flying:* Cultivating physically healthy, intellectually alert, emotionally balanced, and spiritually conscious students.
- **Three-Term Academic Examination Cycle:**
  - *First Term Examination:* September
  - *Second Term Examination:* December
  - *Annual / Final Examination:* March – April
- **Academic Promotion Rules:**
  - 33% – 40% minimum passing mark across subjects
  - Report cards formally signed by parents/guardians at term PTMs
  - Supplementary exam for students not clearing 1–2 subjects
  - Fee clearance required prior to hall-ticket issuance
